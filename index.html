What this chart will never do to your account.

Read-only by design

SCAI reports; it does not operate. Authorization is yours to grant and revoke, the software recommends, and a person takes the action. That is a positioning choice as much as a compliance one, and it is written into how the product is built.

Never creates, edits, pauses, or bids on an advertising campaign.
Never stores a buyer's name, email address, or street address.
Never uses your data to train models, or to serve another customer.
Never keeps collecting after you revoke access in Seller Central; collection stops immediately.
