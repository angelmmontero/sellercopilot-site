Features

Everything a data-driven seller needs, in one place

SCAI ingests your Amazon selling data automatically every day and turns it into dashboards, forecasts, and concrete recommendations — no spreadsheets required.

Sales & Traffic Analytics

Understand demand, not just orders. SCAI analyzes page views, sessions, and conversion alongside revenue, per product and per marketplace.

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly sales dashboards per ASIN
  • Session, conversion rate, and click-through trends
  • Organic vs. advertising sales mix analysis
  • Period-over-period and year-over-year comparisons

Daily Brief & AI Recommendations

Every morning, SCAI evaluates your entire catalog against configurable performance rules and tells you exactly what needs attention — and why.

  • Prioritized daily action plan (ads, inventory, listings, pricing)
  • Evidence panel with the metrics behind every recommendation
  • AI-generated product audits and executive reports
  • Built-in AI assistant that answers questions about your data
  • Outcome tracking: measure the real impact of actions you take

Inventory Forecasting & Order Insights

Never get caught by a stockout. SCAI computes sales velocity from your order history and projects days of supply and reorder dates per product.

  • Days-of-supply forecasts with stockout-risk alerts
  • Recommended reorder dates and quantities
  • Listing availability and Buy Box monitoring
  • Customer journey and repeat-purchase analytics from order data

Profit & Financial Analytics

Know what you actually earn after Amazon fees, refunds, and advertising — per product, not just per account.

  • Net profit per ASIN including FBA and referral fees
  • Transaction-level analysis: refunds, promotions, fee breakdowns
  • TACOS and advertising efficiency tracking against your targets
  • Payout reconciliation and financial trend reports

How it works

From connection to daily insights in three steps

1

Connect your account

Authorize SellerCopilot AI through Amazon's Selling Partner authorization flow. You stay in control and can revoke access at any time.

2

Automatic daily ingestion

SCAI retrieves your sales, traffic, inventory, order, and financial data every day through Amazon's official Selling Partner API.

3

Act on your Daily Brief

Open your morning brief, review prioritized recommendations with their supporting evidence, and track the measured outcome of every action.

Security

Your data is yours. We treat it that way.

Encryption in transit and at rest

All traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS. Amazon API credentials are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Amazon Data Protection Policy compliance

Data obtained through Amazon's APIs is handled in accordance with Amazon's Acceptable Use Policy and Data Protection Policy.

Strict account isolation

Every customer's data is scoped to their own account at the application layer. Your data is never shared with, or visible to, other customers.

You can leave anytime

Disconnect your Amazon authorization at any time and request deletion of your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Reviewing us for your security team? Our full security overview covers encryption, account isolation, how AI features handle your data, and the buyer data we deliberately don't store.
