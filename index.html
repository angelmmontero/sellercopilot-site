Advertising Performance & Ad Spend Efficiency

SCAI reads your Sponsored Products performance every day and joins it to real profit, so you can see which campaigns pay for themselves and which ones quietly eat the margin.

  • Daily Sponsored Products metrics per ASIN and per campaign: impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales and units
  • ACOS and TACOS tracked against the target you set, per product and per portfolio
  • Recommendations to scale, reduce, or stop spend based on net profit after Amazon fees, not on ad revenue alone

  • Read-only by design: SCAI reports on your campaigns and never creates, edits, pauses, or bids on them

What this chart will never do to your account.

Read-only by design

SCAI reports; it does not operate. Authorization is yours to grant and revoke, the software recommends, and a person takes the action. That is a positioning choice as much as a compliance one, and it is written into how the product is built.

Read the security overview
  • Never creates, edits, pauses, or bids on an advertising campaign.
  • Never stores a buyer's name, email address, or street address.
  • Never uses your data to train models, or to serve another customer.
  • Never keeps collecting after you revoke access in Seller Central; collection stops immediately.

Your data is yours. We treat it that way.

Controls in place today

Encryption in transit and at rest

All traffic is served over HTTPS/TLS. Amazon API credentials are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Amazon Data Protection Policy compliance

Data obtained through Amazon's APIs is handled in accordance with Amazon's Acceptable Use Policy and Data Protection Policy.

Strict account isolation

Every customer's data is scoped to their own account at the application layer. Your data is never shared with, or visible to, other customers.

You can leave anytime

Disconnect your Amazon authorization at any time and request deletion of your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
